Privacy Policy - Cutie Patootie

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Data we collect
The Bot only processes the data needed to function: Discord user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and the commands and settings you use with the Bot. The Bot does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, passwords, or other sensitive personal information.

2. How we use data
Data is used only to operate the Bot's features and keep your settings working. We do not sell data or share it with third parties.

3. Data retention
Data is kept only as long as needed for the Bot to work, for example while the Bot is in your server. When the Bot is removed from a server, its data can be deleted on request.

The Bot is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement.
